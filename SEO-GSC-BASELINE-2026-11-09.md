## Ranking-protection BLOCKERS
1. Retain topical/homepage H1 about بطحاء الرياض وتيماء والرمل الأبيض في مكة; draft generic H1 is NOT approved.
2. Preserve or carefully refine high-value Title/Meta, page-body content, headings, internal links on highest-click pages.
3. Keep URLs, working legacy English paths and canonical/hreflang mapping stable.
4. Restore verified Ads and analytics tracking; draft lost legacy GTM / AW tags, and legacy AR/EN had different GTM containers.
5. Confirm noindex staging logic cannot affect production.
6. Test core mobile templates and contact form. 82% of clicks came from mobile.
7. Run Lighthouse/CWV, browser visual checks, structured data validation and JS-disabled crawlability.
8. A redesign may trigger recrawl and shifts, but does not reset the site's index if URLs remain stable. No guarantee of preserving exact position.
9. Export query-filtered *Pages* report for terms "بطحاء الرياض مكة", "بطحاء الرياض", "سعر بطحاء الرياض" to identify exact landing URLs; not infer query-to-page attribution from separate tabs.
10. Keep the current main untouched until owner approves written parity audit and tracking verification.
