Riyadh Bat7a - The Perfect Solution for Plastering and Construction

Material specifications and availability are confirmed before ordering for Makkah projects.

Riyadh Bat7a is one of the most important essential building materials provided by Desert Sands Establishment with the highest standards of quality and purity. Riyadh Bat7a is distinguished by its natural color and smooth texture, making it the ideal choice for many construction applications, especially plastering and render work.

We guarantee to provide Riyadh Bat7a free from impurities and organic materials, which is carefully processed to ensure its consistency and strength when mixed with other materials. Whether you are building a residential, commercial, or industrial project, our Riyadh Bat7a provides the solid and reliable foundation you need.

Features of Riyadh Bat7a

  • High quality and superior purity, free from harmful impurities and materials.
  • Ideal for plastering and render work, ensuring smooth and durable surfaces.
  • Helps provide a homogeneous and strong mix when used in mortar.
  • Regularly available in large quantities to meet the needs of large and small projects.
  • Fast and reliable delivery to all parts of Makkah and surrounding areas.
  • Competitive prices that meet your budget without compromising on quality.

Common Uses of Riyadh Bat7a

  • Plastering (Liyaasah): To create a smooth and level layer on walls and ceilings before painting.
  • Building Mortar: As an essential part of the building mortar for fixing bricks and cement blocks.
  • Light Concrete: In some types of lightweight concrete that require fine aggregate.
  • Finishing Works: To achieve an excellent, crack-resistant final look.
  • Soil Modification: Sometimes used to improve soil properties in some applications.
