Flexible Payment Options for Your Building Material Purchases

Learn about the flexible and secure payment methods provided by Desert Sand Est. for purchasing sand, bat7a, dafan, concrete, and all building materials in Makkah. Cash, bank transfer, Mada, STC Pay, and more.

Cash on Delivery of Building Materials

You can pay in cash upon receiving the service or product (such as sand or bat7a). This method is ideal for those who prefer direct transactions and immediate payment for the supply of building materials.

  • Ease: Quick and direct payment at the site.
