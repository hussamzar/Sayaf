Tartasha - Superior Protection and Thermal Insulation for Surfaces and Walls

Material specifications and availability are confirmed before ordering for Makkah projects.

Tartasha is an essential material in waterproofing and thermal insulation works, providing a durable protective layer for surfaces and walls. Desert Sand Est. provides it in high quality to ensure effective insulation and resistance to harsh weather conditions in Makkah.

Tartasha is characterized by its insulating properties that help maintain the building's temperature and reduce energy consumption for cooling or heating. It also acts as an excellent barrier against water leakage, protecting the building's structural integrity from moisture damage.

Advantages of Tartasha from Desert Sand Est.

  • Effective thermal and moisture insulation for surfaces and walls.
  • High resistance to humidity and temperature changes.
  • Helps rationalize energy consumption inside buildings.
  • Gives surfaces extra strength and rigidity.
  • Easy to apply and adhere to different surfaces.
  • Available in large quantities to suit all project sizes.
  • Fast and reliable delivery to work sites.

Common Uses of Tartasha

  • Surface Insulation: As an initial layer for waterproofing and thermal insulation of surfaces before installing final layers.
  • Exterior Wall Insulation: To protect walls from weather factors and moisture.
  • Concrete Floors: To improve floor insulation and provide a protective layer.
  • Base for Plastering: To ensure better adhesion for subsequent plaster layers.
  • Industrial and Commercial Buildings: To provide extra protection and effective insulation.
