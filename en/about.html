About Us – Desert Sand Est.: Trusted Expertise in Building Material Supply

Our Story: Who We Are at Desert Sand

Desert Sand Est. was founded in 2015 in the heart of Makkah, driven by our firm belief in the importance of providing high-quality building materials to support both major and minor construction projects in the region. Over many years, we have built our reputation as a reliable supplier of building materials on the foundations of trust, consistent quality, and quick responsiveness to the needs of our valued clients. We understand that every project, big or small, represents an investment in the future, and thus we are committed to always delivering the best in every aspect of our work.

Our Vision and Mission

Our Vision: To become the first choice and most reliable supplier of building materials in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, through continuous innovation and our firm commitment to the highest international quality standards in everything we provide, from white sand, bat7a, and dafan.

Our Mission: To provide a wide range of essential building materials with the highest quality standards, supported by efficient and fast delivery, to enable our clients to successfully complete their projects with peace of mind, with a focus on providing the best price for sand and all materials.

Our Core Values

  • Quality First: We never compromise on the quality of our products. Every material we supply undergoes strict quality control to ensure it meets the most precise standard specifications for construction.
  • Reliability: We honor our commitments to delivery and specified deadlines, ensuring your projects run smoothly without any unwanted delays.
  • Exceptional Customer Service: Our clients are at the heart of our focus, and we strive to provide continuous support and quick, effective responses to all their inquiries and needs.
  • Professionalism: Our team is highly qualified and trained to provide the best services, from specialized consultations to safe and efficient delivery.

We are proud to be an integral part of Makkah's urban and developmental renaissance, and we look forward to building strong and long-term partnerships with you as your preferred supplier for all your building sand and bat7a needs.
